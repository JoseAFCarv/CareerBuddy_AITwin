🚀 Career Buddy: AI-Driven Career Transformation
Finalist Project – Dean’s Open Innovation Challenge 2026

Developed at NOVA IMS (Information Management School) under the "Data Visionaries" theme.

📖 Project Overview
Career Buddy is a proactive ecosystem designed to navigate the uncertainties of an AI-impacted workforce . By leveraging the concept of a "Digital Career Twin," the platform bridges the gap between current professional skills and future market demands . It moves beyond reactive job searching to offer personalized, data-driven career pathfinding .

🏆 Achievement Highlights
•	Finalist: Selected as a top-tier project for the final pitch phase among all competing teams. 
•	Pitch Day: Presented the solution to a high-level jury, including the NOVA IMS Director and industry leaders.

🎯 Strategic Objectives

•	Predictive Upskilling: Analyzing job APIs, patent filings, and scientific innovations to forecast skill demand before it peaks, giving users a competitive edge .
•	Data Integration: Merging explicit data (resumes), implicit data (personality/passions), and external market analysis into a unified user profile .
•	Scalable Impact: A robust B2C and B2B business model aimed at institutional HR and lifelong human development .
